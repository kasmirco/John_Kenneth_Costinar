Spanish Colonial Exhibit

The Spanish Colonial Exhibit is an interactive cultural heritage website designed to help users explore the history and influence of the Spanish colonial era in the Philippines. It presents historical stories, culture, fashion, food, and music through a visually engaging experience, with interactive content designed to make learning more accessible and memorable.
