Campus Lost & Found System

Lost & Found

A safer and more organized way to reconnect students with their belongings.

FEU Lost & Found is an application built exclusively for FEU students and staff to manage lost and recovered items within the campus. It features an intuitive interface for reporting lost belongings, tracking found items, and coordinating returns through the university’s Lost & Found office. The platform is designed to provide secure and easy access while helping the FEU community manage lost and found items more efficiently.

Key Features

  • Item Reporting: Quickly report lost or found items.
  • Real-Time Tracking: Monitor the status of reported items and updates.
  • Secure Access: Restricted to FEU students and staff to ensure privacy and authenticity.
